# Project logo artwork

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool on September 17, 2026, after Mason chose sculptural marks with depth and shading. Each design is a separate transparent image.

The website uses optimized 512 × 512 WebP assets in `public/projects/logos/`, preserving transparency. Keeping them in this directory prevents the existing screenshot scripts from overwriting them. The SVG concepts remain available as earlier source artwork. Original generation files remain in the local ImageGen output directory.

## Final prompts

### nova-core

Asset: `public/projects/logos/nova-core.webp`

```text
Use case: stylized-concept / logo-brand.
Asset type: final standalone sculptural logo for Nova Core, an eight-agent AI platform, used at roughly 130px on a dark portfolio card.
Create ONE original premium three-dimensional brand symbol: an intricately folded eight-part stellar core. Eight tapered architectural fins interlock around a small octagonal void, each fin twisting between a sharp outer point and a broad inner facet. The silhouette is a compact asymmetric-looking star turbine with rigorous eight-part structure, not a flat asterisk or loading spinner. Show the ingenious over-and-under assembly in true sculptural depth.
Materials: satin lavender anodized titanium on broad faces, pale platinum polished bevels, dark violet recessed sidewalls, very subtle hairline machining. Physically believable reflections and occlusion. This is an art-directed industrial design object, not a toy.
Composition: square canvas, one isolated mark occupying about 78% of the square, centered with comfortable margins; shallow three-quarter view so the full face reads clearly. No pedestal or ground plane. Soft broad studio key from upper left and a restrained rim highlight reveal facets.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha, including the opening in the center. Edges should work on a charcoal background.
No text, no labels, no wordmark, no square app tile, no orbit rings, no extra props, no flat vector styling, no glowing aura, no watermark.
```

### aeris

Asset: `public/projects/logos/aeris.webp`

```text
Use case: stylized-concept / logo-brand.
Asset type: final standalone sculptural logo for A.E.R.I.S., which verifies air-quality claims against sensor data, displayed at roughly 130px on a dark portfolio card.
Create ONE original premium three-dimensional symbol: a flowing open capital A built from three layered aerodynamic ribbons. The left leg rises in a taut elegant arc, folds over a rounded narrow apex, and flows down toward a longer swept right tail; a second smaller wave crosses inside the form, with a third recessed fin giving depth. It should evoke measured air currents and an instrument sculpted by the wind. Maintain a strong distinctive A silhouette with large readable negative spaces.
Materials: substantial frosted aquamarine optical glass with translucent turquoise edges, softly pearlescent inner faces and dark teal shading. Thick polished edges reveal the actual physical thickness. Beautiful controlled refraction, not an all-clear invisible glass blob. Subtle shifts in depth between the ribbons. No glitter.
Composition: square canvas, single isolated sculpture occupying about 78% of the square, shallow three-quarter view, near frontal, centered with generous consistent margins. Soft studio illumination with pale edge highlights, readable against a dark charcoal website.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha. No ground, pedestal, backdrop panel or cast shadow outside the object.
No text, no labels, no wordmark, no app tile, no cloud pictogram, no circle badge, no floating particles, no neon glow, no flat vector, no watermark.
```

### freshtrack

A pair of sculpted sage-green leaves and a short dark bronze stem grow from the top. Integrate the clock into the actual fruit, not a clock icon pasted onto a ball.

```text
Background: genuinely transparent alpha, no ground plane, stand or backdrop. Designed for a charcoal website.
No words, no numerals, no text, no rectangular app tile, no extra food, no hands or people, no watermark.
```

### first-step

Asset: `public/projects/logos/first-step.webp`

```text
Use case: stylized-concept / logo-brand.
Asset type: final standalone sculptural logo for First Step, a student-run environmental nonprofit, displayed at roughly 130px on a dark portfolio card.
Create ONE original premium three-dimensional environmental emblem: a folded leaf whose surface is carved into broad stepped topographic terraces. A winding walking trail is an open S-shaped cut through the middle, leading from a small separate golden stepping stone at the lower-left base up toward the high pointed tip at the upper right. The leaf and terraces form one coherent compact sculpture, a distinctive outdoor conservation identity rather than a generic leaf icon or a landscape diorama.
Materials: deep jade and soft sage ceramic across the terraced upper surfaces; visible earthy terracotta cut edges; very restrained warm bronze highlights. Show tangible thickness, 4 or 5 broad contour steps, small soft bevels and natural occlusion in the trail. Mature handcrafted sculpture, no plastic toy or cartoon styling.
Composition: square canvas; centered isolated mark occupying 78% of the canvas, leaf inclined diagonally upwards, gentle three-quarter camera angle revealing the terraces and hollow trail; broad studio lighting from upper left, enough light in recesses to read on a charcoal website.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha, including trail cutout; no floor, pedestal, mountains, plants or scenery.
No words, no text, no labels, no badge container, no app tile, no floating particles, no glow, no watermark.
```
